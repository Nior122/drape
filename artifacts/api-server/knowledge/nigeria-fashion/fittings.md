# Fittings Checklist Knowledge

Use these checklists to guide clients and tailors through the fitting process.

## 1. First Fitting Checklist
**Goal:** Verify the foundational structure and basic fit before adding finishing touches or heavy embroidery.

- [ ] **Bodice / Chest:** Does it sit flat? Is there pulling or gaping? For corsets, are the cups positioned correctly?
- [ ] **Waistline:** Is it hitting the natural waist? Is it too tight to sit down?
- [ ] **Shoulders:** Does the shoulder seam sit exactly on the edge of the shoulder?
- [ ] **Armhole:** Can the client lift their arms comfortably? Does the armhole cut into the armpit?
- [ ] **Length (Initial):** Is the general length correct? (Pinning for final hem is usually done here).
- [ ] **Fastenings:** Can the zipper close smoothly without straining?

## 2. Second Fitting Checklist
**Goal:** Verify adjustments made from the first fitting and check the drape of the fabric.

- [ ] **Alteration Check:** Were all pinned changes from the first fitting executed correctly?
- [ ] **Drape & Flow:** Does the fabric fall smoothly over the hips and legs?
- [ ] **Sleeve Length:** Are the sleeves hitting the correct spot (wrist bone for long sleeves)?
- [ ] **Neckline:** Does the neckline sit flat against the chest/back without gaping?
- [ ] **Comfort Check:** Ask the client to sit, walk, and raise their arms.

## 3. Final Fitting / Delivery Checklist
**Goal:** Ensure the garment is flawless, pressed, and ready to wear.

- [ ] **Hemming:** Is the hem perfectly level and secure? (Blind stitched if necessary).
- [ ] **Embellishments:** Are all stones, beads, and appliques securely attached?
- [ ] **Finishing (Inside):** Are all inner seams neatened (serged/weaved or bias-bound)? Are there any loose threads?
- [ ] **Fastenings Check:** Do all zippers, buttons, and hooks work perfectly?
- [ ] **Pressing:** Has the garment been professionally ironed/steamed to remove all creases?
- [ ] **Packaging:** Is it hung securely in a Drape garment bag?
