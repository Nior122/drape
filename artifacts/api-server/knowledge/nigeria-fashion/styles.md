# Nigerian Styles Guide

## Women's Styles

### Corset Gown
- **Description:** Structured bodice with boning/lacing that cinches the waist, often paired with a flared or straight skirt.
- **Body Shape Suitability:** Hourglass, Rectangle (creates a waist).
- **Occasion Suitability:** Wedding (Bride/Reception), Red carpet, high-end birthdays.
- **Complexity Level:** Very High (requires precision drafting, boning, and cup structuring).
- **Estimated Production Time:** 3 - 4 weeks.

### Mermaid Gown
- **Description:** Fitted from bodice to knee/mid-thigh, then flares out dramatically.
- **Body Shape Suitability:** Hourglass, Pear. Avoid for Apple.
- **Occasion Suitability:** Red carpet, Weddings, formal events.
- **Complexity Level:** High (fit must be exact around the hips/knees).
- **Estimated Production Time:** 2 - 3 weeks.

### Boubou
- **Description:** A long, wide, flowing dress/robe. Very elegant and comfortable.
- **Body Shape Suitability:** All body types. Very forgiving for Apple and Heavy-set shapes.
- **Occasion Suitability:** Owambe, church events, casual elegance.
- **Complexity Level:** Low.
- **Estimated Production Time:** 3 - 5 days.

### Peplum
- **Description:** Fitted top or dress with a flared ruffle at the waist.
- **Body Shape Suitability:** Pear, Rectangle (creates hip volume).
- **Occasion Suitability:** Office, church, semi-formal.
- **Complexity Level:** Medium.
- **Estimated Production Time:** 1 week.

### Skirt and Blouse
- **Description:** A classic Nigerian two-piece. Blouse styled to match a wrapper or tailored skirt.
- **Body Shape Suitability:** All body types.
- **Occasion Suitability:** Traditional weddings, Owambe, Church.
- **Complexity Level:** Medium.
- **Estimated Production Time:** 1 - 2 weeks.

### Iro and Buba
- **Description:** Traditional Yoruba wrapper (Iro) and loose-fitting blouse (Buba). Modern versions use silk/chiffon ("Oleku" style uses shorter sleeves/wrapper).
- **Body Shape Suitability:** All body types.
- **Occasion Suitability:** Traditional ceremonies, Owambe.
- **Complexity Level:** Low.
- **Estimated Production Time:** 3 - 5 days.

### Kaftan (Women)
- **Description:** A loose, flowing dress, often heavily embroidered at the neckline.
- **Body Shape Suitability:** All body types. Flattering for plus-size.
- **Occasion Suitability:** Eid, casual wear, simple events.
- **Complexity Level:** Low to Medium (depending on embroidery).
- **Estimated Production Time:** 1 week (longer if hand-embroidered).

### Reception Gown
- **Description:** A bride's second outfit. Usually lighter and more party-ready than the wedding gown. Often features skin net, crystals, and a slit.
- **Body Shape Suitability:** Varies by chosen silhouette.
- **Occasion Suitability:** Wedding reception.
- **Complexity Level:** Very High.
- **Estimated Production Time:** 4 - 6 weeks.

## Men's Styles

### Agbada
- **Description:** Traditional 3-piece: wide outer robe, inner top (buba), and trousers (sokoto). Heavy embroidery on the chest.
- **Body Shape Suitability:** All body types. Large men look regal.
- **Occasion Suitability:** Traditional weddings, chieftaincy events, high-end parties.
- **Complexity Level:** High (mainly due to intricate embroidery).
- **Estimated Production Time:** 2 - 3 weeks.

### Senator
- **Description:** A two-piece native suit — long top with matching trousers. Clean lines, minimal embroidery.
- **Body Shape Suitability:** All body types. Slim/Athletic look sharpest.
- **Occasion Suitability:** Church, corporate, parties, casual smart.
- **Complexity Level:** Medium (requires precise trouser tailoring).
- **Estimated Production Time:** 1 - 2 weeks.

### Kaftan (Men)
- **Description:** A long flowing robe reaching the ankles.
- **Body Shape Suitability:** All body types.
- **Occasion Suitability:** Eid, casual events, Friday prayers.
- **Complexity Level:** Low.
- **Estimated Production Time:** 5 - 7 days.

### Isi Agu
- **Description:** Igbo traditional tunic featuring lion-head prints, worn with plain trousers and a red cap.
- **Body Shape Suitability:** All body types.
- **Occasion Suitability:** Igbo traditional weddings, chieftaincy, funerals.
- **Complexity Level:** Medium.
- **Estimated Production Time:** 1 week.

### Native Wear (General)
- **Description:** Any two-piece traditional outfit (e.g., South-South attire with a bowler hat and walking stick).
- **Body Shape Suitability:** All body types.
- **Occasion Suitability:** Traditional events across regions.
- **Complexity Level:** Medium.
- **Estimated Production Time:** 1 - 2 weeks.

### Corporate Suit
- **Description:** Western-style tailored suit (jacket and trousers).
- **Body Shape Suitability:** All body types (when bespoke).
- **Occasion Suitability:** Corporate events, white weddings.
- **Complexity Level:** Very High (canvas structure, lapel rolling, exact fit).
- **Estimated Production Time:** 3 - 5 weeks.
