# Nigerian Occasions Knowledge

## Wedding Events

### Traditional Wedding (Introduction / Igba Nkwu)
- **Formality Level:** High / Regal
- **Recommended Colors:** Rich, vibrant colours (Burgundy, Emerald Green, Gold, Royal Blue, Coral). Usually dictated by the family "colours of the day".
- **Recommended Fabrics:** Aso-Oke, George, premium Lace, heavily embellished Velvet.
- **Recommended Styles:** 
  - *Women:* Iro and Buba, Skirt and Blouse, heavily beaded Corset gowns, elaborate Gele.
  - *Men:* Agbada, South-South wrapper and top, Isi Agu.

### White Wedding
- **Formality Level:** High / Formal
- **Recommended Colors:** White/Ivory (for bride), Black, Navy, Grey (for men). Guests usually wear Aso-Ebi colours or pastels.
- **Recommended Fabrics:** Satin, Tulle, Organza, Silk, fine Wool/Cashmere (for men).
- **Recommended Styles:**
  - *Women:* Bridal gowns, Reception gowns, elegant evening gowns (for guests).
  - *Men:* Corporate suits, Tuxedos.

## Celebrations & Milestones

### Birthday / Owambe
- **Formality Level:** Medium to High (Glamorous)
- **Recommended Colors:** Bold and trendy (Fuchsia, Burnt Orange, Metallic tones).
- **Recommended Fabrics:** Ankara, Sequins, Lace, Crepe.
- **Recommended Styles:** 
  - *Women:* Peplum, Mermaid gowns, short trendy dresses, Boubou.
  - *Men:* Senator, Agbada (for milestone birthdays like 50th/60th).

### Naming Ceremony
- **Formality Level:** Medium (Smart Traditional)
- **Recommended Colors:** Soft, joyful colours (White, Powder Blue, Baby Pink, Gold).
- **Recommended Fabrics:** White Lace, fine Cotton, Guinea Brocade, Ankara.
- **Recommended Styles:** 
  - *Women:* Kaftan, Boubou, simple Iro and Buba.
  - *Men:* Simple Agbada, Senator.

### Graduation
- **Formality Level:** Medium (Smart Casual to Semi-Formal)
- **Recommended Colors:** Navy, Black, White, or school colours.
- **Recommended Fabrics:** Crepe, Cotton, Linen, Wool blends.
- **Recommended Styles:**
  - *Women:* Fitted gowns, Skirt and Blouse, Jumpsuits.
  - *Men:* Corporate suit, smart Senator.

## Religious & Cultural

### Church Event / Thanksgiving
- **Formality Level:** Medium (Modest and Dignified)
- **Recommended Colors:** White (especially for Thanksgiving), pastels, conservative deep tones.
- **Recommended Fabrics:** Lace, Ankara, Crepe, Chiffon.
- **Recommended Styles:**
  - *Women:* Boubou, Peplum, long fitted gowns (modest necklines, covered shoulders).
  - *Men:* Senator, Corporate suit.

### Eid / Sallah
- **Formality Level:** Medium to High (Modest and Flowing)
- **Recommended Colors:** White, Cream, Gold, deep jewel tones.
- **Recommended Fabrics:** Brocade, polished Cotton, Silk, Chiffon.
- **Recommended Styles:**
  - *Women:* Flowing Kaftans, Abayas, Boubous.
  - *Men:* Flowing Kaftans, Agbada.

### Burial Ceremony
- **Formality Level:** Medium to High (Respectful)
- **Recommended Colors:** Black, White, or the family's chosen Aso-Ebi (often dark red, navy, or brown).
- **Recommended Fabrics:** Lace, Ankara, Aso-Oke.
- **Recommended Styles:**
  - *Women:* Iro and Buba, modest gowns, Skirt and Blouse.
  - *Men:* Agbada, Senator.

## Professional & Everyday

### Corporate Event
- **Formality Level:** High (Professional)
- **Recommended Colors:** Navy, Charcoal, Black, Grey, muted tones.
- **Recommended Fabrics:** Wool, Crepe, high-quality Cotton.
- **Recommended Styles:**
  - *Women:* Fitted gowns, tailored suits, pencil skirts.
  - *Men:* Corporate suits, highly tailored minimalistic Senator.

### Photoshoot
- **Formality Level:** Varies completely based on the theme.
- **Recommended Colors:** Ask the client for their theme.
- **Recommended Fabrics:** Mesh, Tulle, Sequins, bold Ankara prints.
- **Recommended Styles:** Anything from avant-garde to traditional. Ask for the mood board.
