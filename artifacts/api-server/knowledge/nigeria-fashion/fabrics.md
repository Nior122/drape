# Nigerian Fabrics Guide

When recommending a fabric, explain:
1. Why it suits the client's occasion
2. Comfort level (hot/cool, breathable)
3. Weather suitability (Nigerian heat vs AC)
4. Care instructions

---

### Ankara
- **Common Nigerian Name:** Ankara, Wax Print
- **Description:** Colourful wax-printed cotton fabric. Iconic Nigerian fabric.
- **Best Use Cases:** Casual wear, party outfits, skirt and blouse, casual gowns.
- **Weather Suitability:** Excellent for Nigerian heat. Breathable.
- **Price Range:** ₦3,000 - ₦15,000 per 6 yards.
- **Care Instructions:** Hand wash or gentle machine wash in cold water. Iron on reverse. Do not bleach.
- **Typical Client Category:** Budget to Mid-range. Everyday wearers.

### Lace
- **Common Nigerian Name:** Lace (Dry lace, Swiss voile, French lace, Cord lace, Net lace)
- **Description:** Delicate woven fabric with intricate patterns.
- **Best Use Cases:** Owambe, weddings, formal church events.
- **Weather Suitability:** Varies by type. Cotton-based (Swiss voile) is breathable. Polyester-based can be hot. Requires lining.
- **Price Range:** ₦10,000 - ₦500,000+ per 5 yards.
- **Care Instructions:** Dry clean only. Handle with care. Avoid catching on jewellery.
- **Typical Client Category:** Mid-range to Luxury. Wedding guests and celebrants.

### George
- **Common Nigerian Name:** George
- **Description:** Heavy woven fabric, often silk-like, with gold or silver embroidery on the borders.
- **Best Use Cases:** Igbo traditional weddings, Niger Delta ceremonies. Wrapper and blouse styles.
- **Weather Suitability:** Heavy, best for AC environments or evening events.
- **Price Range:** ₦50,000 - ₦300,000+ per wrapper set.
- **Care Instructions:** Dry clean only. Iron on very low heat on reverse side.
- **Typical Client Category:** Luxury. Traditional brides, mothers of the couple.

### Aso Oke
- **Common Nigerian Name:** Aso Oke
- **Description:** Hand-woven Yoruba fabric. Thick and structured.
- **Best Use Cases:** Yoruba traditional weddings, Agbada, Gele (headtie), Ipele (stole).
- **Weather Suitability:** Very heavy and hot. Worn ceremonially, not for comfort.
- **Price Range:** ₦30,000 - ₦200,000+ depending on weave and beading.
- **Care Instructions:** Dry clean only. Do not wash at home.
- **Typical Client Category:** Mid-range to Luxury. Traditional celebrants.

### Adire
- **Common Nigerian Name:** Adire, Kampala (sometimes used interchangeably)
- **Description:** Yoruba tie-dye fabric. Usually indigo blue patterns on white or cream.
- **Best Use Cases:** Casual wear, boubou, matching sets, cultural events.
- **Weather Suitability:** Light cotton, excellent for heat.
- **Price Range:** ₦8,000 - ₦25,000 per 5 yards.
- **Care Instructions:** Hand wash separately in cold water with mild soap. Colour may run initially.
- **Typical Client Category:** Budget to Mid-range.

### Kampala
- **Common Nigerian Name:** Kampala
- **Description:** Northern Nigerian tie-dye/batik fabric. Often vibrant and multi-coloured.
- **Best Use Cases:** Casual kaftans, boubous, everyday wear.
- **Weather Suitability:** Breathable cotton, great for warm weather.
- **Price Range:** ₦5,000 - ₦15,000 per 5 yards.
- **Care Instructions:** Hand wash separately.
- **Typical Client Category:** Budget to Mid-range.

### Crepe
- **Common Nigerian Name:** Crepe
- **Description:** Slightly textured matte fabric. Drapes well and holds shape. Has slight stretch.
- **Best Use Cases:** Fitted gowns, corporate wear, jumpsuits, draped styles.
- **Weather Suitability:** Comfortable in most weather. Drapes nicely without clinging.
- **Price Range:** ₦3,000 - ₦10,000 per yard.
- **Care Instructions:** Dry clean or gentle hand wash. Do not wring.
- **Typical Client Category:** Mid-range. Corporate and party guests.

### Velvet
- **Common Nigerian Name:** Velvet
- **Description:** Soft, plush fabric with a rich texture.
- **Best Use Cases:** Evening gowns, Agbada accents, December parties.
- **Weather Suitability:** Warm and heavy. Best for Harmattan or AC venues.
- **Price Range:** ₦5,000 - ₦25,000 per yard.
- **Care Instructions:** Dry clean only. Never iron directly on the pile; steam from the inside.
- **Typical Client Category:** Mid-range to Luxury.

### Organza
- **Common Nigerian Name:** Organza
- **Description:** Thin, sheer, slightly stiff fabric.
- **Best Use Cases:** Puffy sleeves, overlays, bridal details.
- **Weather Suitability:** Lightweight but can be scratchy. Usually layered.
- **Price Range:** ₦2,000 - ₦8,000 per yard.
- **Care Instructions:** Gentle hand wash or dry clean. Iron on low heat.
- **Typical Client Category:** Budget to Mid-range.

### Tulle
- **Common Nigerian Name:** Net / Tulle
- **Description:** Soft mesh fabric, used for volume.
- **Best Use Cases:** Ball gowns, bridal skirts, illusion necklines (skin net).
- **Weather Suitability:** Breathable, but requires lining.
- **Price Range:** ₦1,000 - ₦5,000 per yard (skin illusion net is pricier).
- **Care Instructions:** Hand wash gently.
- **Typical Client Category:** Mid-range to Luxury (brides).

### Silk
- **Common Nigerian Name:** Silk (Duchess silk, raw silk)
- **Description:** Natural luxury fabric. Soft, smooth, with a subtle sheen.
- **Best Use Cases:** High-end formal wear, bridal outfits, luxury kaftans.
- **Weather Suitability:** Very comfortable, regulates temperature well.
- **Price Range:** ₦8,000 - ₦30,000+ per yard.
- **Care Instructions:** Dry clean only.
- **Typical Client Category:** Luxury.

### Cotton
- **Common Nigerian Name:** Plain Cotton / Guinea Brocade (if textured)
- **Description:** Natural breathable fabric.
- **Best Use Cases:** Men's Senator styles, simple kaftans.
- **Weather Suitability:** Excellent for Nigerian heat.
- **Price Range:** ₦2,000 - ₦15,000 per yard.
- **Care Instructions:** Machine or hand wash. Irons well.
- **Typical Client Category:** Budget to Mid-range.

### Linen
- **Common Nigerian Name:** Linen
- **Description:** Natural fabric made from flax. Lightweight and slightly textured. Wrinkles easily.
- **Best Use Cases:** Men's native wear, casual two-piece sets.
- **Weather Suitability:** Excellent for heat. Very breathable.
- **Price Range:** ₦5,000 - ₦15,000 per yard.
- **Care Instructions:** Wash gently. Must be ironed with steam.
- **Typical Client Category:** Mid-range.
