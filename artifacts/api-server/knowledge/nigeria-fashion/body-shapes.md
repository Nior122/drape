# Body Shape Knowledge

## Women's Body Shapes

### Pear (Triangle)
- **Characteristics:** Hips are wider than the bust and shoulders. Well-defined waist.
- **Recommended Styles:** A-line gowns, Fit and flare, Peplum tops, Boat necklines, Off-shoulder styles (to balance shoulders with hips), Mermaid gowns (if comfortable highlighting hips).
- **Styles to Avoid:** Clingy fabrics on the lower half without structure, overly tight pencil skirts, dropped waistlines.
- **Fitting Recommendations:** Ensure the waist is well-cinched to highlight the narrowest part. Skirts should flow over the hips or be tailored perfectly if fitted.

### Apple (Inverted Oval)
- **Characteristics:** Broader shoulders and bust, narrower hips, less defined waist. Weight is carried around the midsection.
- **Recommended Styles:** Boubou, Kaftan, Empire waist gowns, V-necklines (to elongate), Wrap dresses, A-line skirts.
- **Styles to Avoid:** Tight corset gowns, heavily pleated waistlines, stiff fabrics around the midsection, high necklines.
- **Fitting Recommendations:** Focus on drawing attention upward to the face and neckline or downward to the legs. Ensure fabrics drape nicely over the midsection without clinging.

### Hourglass
- **Characteristics:** Bust and hips are roughly equal in size, with a well-defined, narrow waist.
- **Recommended Styles:** Corset gowns, Mermaid gowns, Fitted gowns, Wrap dresses, Pencil skirts, Peplum.
- **Styles to Avoid:** Oversized, shapeless garments (unless styled intentionally, like a rich boubou), stiff fabrics that hide the waist.
- **Fitting Recommendations:** The key is to highlight the waist. Tailoring should follow the natural curves without pulling.

### Rectangle (Straight)
- **Characteristics:** Shoulders, waist, and hips are roughly the same width. Little to no waist definition.
- **Recommended Styles:** Corset gowns (to create a waist), Peplum (to add hip volume), Pleated skirts, Wrap dresses, Styles with belts or waist cinching.
- **Styles to Avoid:** Shapeless shifts, very tight vertical dresses that highlight the straight lines.
- **Fitting Recommendations:** Use structure, padding, or fabric draping to create the illusion of curves.

### Inverted Triangle
- **Characteristics:** Broad shoulders, narrow hips and waist.
- **Recommended Styles:** A-line skirts, Peplum, V-necklines, Halter necks, Trousers with wide legs or details, Mermaid gowns with dramatic flares.
- **Styles to Avoid:** Shoulder pads, boat necklines, puff sleeves, skinny trousers that exaggerate narrow hips.
- **Fitting Recommendations:** Keep the top simple and add volume or detail to the lower half to balance the broad shoulders.

## Men's Body Shapes

### Slim / Rectangular
- **Characteristics:** Straight up and down, little variation between shoulders, waist, and hips.
- **Recommended Styles:** Senator, tailored suits, fitted Agbada (not excessively wide), layered clothing.
- **Styles to Avoid:** Extremely baggy clothes that swallow the frame, very tight clothes.
- **Fitting Recommendations:** Use slightly stiffer fabrics (like linen or thick cotton) to add structure and bulk to the frame.

### Athletic / Trapezoid
- **Characteristics:** Broad shoulders and chest, narrower waist and hips.
- **Recommended Styles:** Fitted Senator styles, tailored suits, Agbada, Kaftan. Almost all styles work well.
- **Styles to Avoid:** Very loose, shapeless garments that hide the physique.
- **Fitting Recommendations:** Ensure the chest area has enough room without being baggy. Trousers should be tailored but not skinny.

### Broad / Inverted Triangle
- **Characteristics:** Very broad shoulders and chest, narrow waist. Often heavily muscled.
- **Recommended Styles:** V-neck Kaftans, well-tailored Senator suits with unstructured shoulders.
- **Styles to Avoid:** Shoulder pads, tight shirts that pull across the chest, skinny trousers.
- **Fitting Recommendations:** Avoid adding bulk to the shoulders. Ensure enough room in the upper arms and chest for movement.

### Heavy Set / Oval
- **Characteristics:** Broader midsection, narrower shoulders, and legs.
- **Recommended Styles:** Agbada (excellent for adding regal structure and hiding midsection), flowing Kaftans, Senator styles in dark colors.
- **Styles to Avoid:** Tight, clingy fabrics, horizontal stripes, double-breasted suits.
- **Fitting Recommendations:** Use vertical lines. Ensure trousers sit comfortably at the natural waist, not underneath the belly. Fabrics should drape smoothly.
