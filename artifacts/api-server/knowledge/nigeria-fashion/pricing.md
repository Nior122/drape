# Nigerian Fashion Pricing Estimation System

## Rules for AI Estimation
- **Always state:** "Final price should be confirmed by the producer."
- Base pricing depends heavily on the designer, but use these general ranges to guide the client's expectations.
- Explain *why* something costs what it does (e.g., hand beading, premium fabric, express delivery).
- If a client's budget is too low, suggest a simpler alternative rather than just saying no.

## Estimation Inputs
When calculating an estimate, consider:
1. **Style Complexity:** Simple (straight sewing), Medium (some structure, peplum, pockets), High (corsetry, boning, draping), Very High (hand-beading, couture techniques).
2. **Fabric Type:** Cotton/Ankara (easy), Crepe/Linen (moderate), Lace/Silk/Velvet (difficult), Aso-Oke (requires specific handling).
3. **Embellishments:** None, machine embroidery, hand-beading, stoning, appliques.
4. **Delivery Urgency:** Standard (3-4 weeks), Rush (1-2 weeks), Express (Under 1 week).

## Estimation Outputs (Labour Range & Timeline)

### Low to Mid-Range
- **Profile:** Simple styles, basic fabric, no embellishments.
- **Estimated Labour Range:** ₦20,000 - ₦80,000
- **Estimated Timeline:** 1 - 2 weeks
- **Examples:** Simple Ankara dresses, basic Kaftans, plain Senator styles.

### Mid to High-Range
- **Profile:** Medium complexity, moderate structure, good fabric, minimal embellishments.
- **Estimated Labour Range:** ₦80,000 - ₦250,000
- **Estimated Timeline:** 2 - 3 weeks
- **Examples:** Aso-Ebi styles, embroidered Senator, simple Agbada, basic corsets.

### Premium / Luxury
- **Profile:** High/Very High complexity, premium fabrics, heavy embellishments, corsetry.
- **Estimated Labour Range:** ₦250,000 - ₦1,000,000+
- **Estimated Timeline:** 3 - 6 weeks
- **Examples:** Bridal wear, full hand-beaded Agbada, bespoke European suits.

## Adjustments
- **Urgency:** If the client needs it Express (under 1 week), add 30-50% to the estimated labour range, and explicitly warn them that rush fees apply.
- **Fabric:** If working with Silk, Velvet, or delicate Lace, lean towards the higher end of the range.
