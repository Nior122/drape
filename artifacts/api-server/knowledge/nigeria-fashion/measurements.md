# Measurements Guide

## Rules
- NEVER guess measurements.
- NEVER accept "medium" or "large" as a measurement — always ask for actual numbers.
- Ask politely if measurements are missing.
- If the client says they don't have measurements, tell them how to take them simply.
- Measurements should be in centimetres (cm) or inches (in) — confirm which unit the client prefers.

---

## Women's Measurements (8 points)

| # | Measurement | How to Take |
|---|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | **Bust** | Around the fullest part of the chest |
| 2 | **Under Bust** | Directly under the bust/chest |
| 3 | **Waist** | Around the narrowest part of the waist (usually above the belly button) |
| 4 | **Hip** | Around the fullest part of the hips/buttocks |
| 5 | **Shoulder** | From one shoulder tip to the other, across the back |
| 6 | **Arm Round** | Around the fullest part of the upper arm |
| 7 | **Sleeve Length** | From shoulder tip to where the sleeve should end |
| 8 | **Dress Length** | From the shoulder or waist to where the outfit should end |

---

## Men's Measurements (8 points)

| # | Measurement | How to Take |
|---|-------------|-------------|
| 1 | **Chest** | Around the fullest part of the chest |
| 2 | **Waist** | Around the natural waist |
| 3 | **Hip** | Around the fullest part of the seat |
| 4 | **Shoulder** | From one shoulder tip to the other |
| 5 | **Neck** | Around the base of the neck |
| 6 | **Sleeve Length** | From shoulder tip to wrist |
| 7 | **Trouser Length** | From the natural waist down to the ankle or preferred length |
| 8 | **Thigh** | Around the fullest part of the upper thigh |

---

## How to Ask for Measurements (Good Examples)

"I'll need your measurements to make sure this fits perfectly. Do you have them handy?"

"Could you share your bust, waist, and hip measurements? If you're not sure how to measure, I can guide you step by step."

"I don't want to guess — measurements make all the difference. Can you grab a tape measure?"

---

## What to Do If Client Has No Measurements

Say this:
"No problem! Here's how to take your measurements at home — you'll just need a tape measure and someone to help if possible."

Then guide them through the 3 most critical measurements for their outfit type:
- For a top/dress: Bust, Waist, Hip
- For trousers: Waist, Hip, Thigh, Length
- For a full outfit: All measurements
