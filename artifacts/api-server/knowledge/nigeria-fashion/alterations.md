# Alterations Knowledge

Use this module when clients request changes after a fitting or when creating alteration instructions for the tailor.

## Common Alteration Requests

### 1. Reduce Waist (Take In)
- **Scenario:** The garment is loose around the midsection.
- **Tailor Instructions:** 
  - Unpick the side seams around the waist area.
  - Pin the excess fabric to the client's desired tightness during fitting.
  - Taper the new seam smoothly into the existing bust and hip seams so there are no sharp angles ("dog ears").
  - Restitch, press the seam flat.

### 2. Increase Bust/Hips (Let Out)
- **Scenario:** The garment is too tight.
- **Tailor Instructions:**
  - Check the seam allowance (inward fabric reserve). Nigerian tailors usually leave 1.5 to 2 inches of allowance.
  - Unpick the side seams.
  - Sew a new seam closer to the fabric edge by the required amount (e.g., let out by 1 inch total = 0.5 inch on each side).
  - Press the new seam flat. If allowance is insufficient, a gusset (matching fabric panel) must be inserted.

### 3. Shorten Sleeve / Adjust Length
- **Scenario:** Sleeves are too long or skirt/trouser drags on the floor.
- **Tailor Instructions:**
  - Client must wear the exact shoes they plan to wear with the outfit during the fitting.
  - Pin the hem to the desired length.
  - Unpick the original hem. Cut excess fabric leaving 1 inch for folding.
  - Hem manually (blind stitch for formal wear, machine stitch for casual wear) or use hemming gum for a seamless look.

### 4. Tighten Fit (Overall Tapering)
- **Scenario:** The client wants a more fitted, body-hugging silhouette.
- **Tailor Instructions:**
  - Pin the garment along the side seams from the armhole down to the hem.
  - If it's a corset or structured bodice, adjust from the back zipper or lacing panel first to maintain the front design symmetry.
  - Sew along the pinned line, taper smoothly.

### 5. Adjust Shoulder / Armhole
- **Scenario:** The shoulder drops too low, or the armhole cuts into the armpit.
- **Tailor Instructions:**
  - *Shoulder too wide:* Unpick the sleeve, trim the shoulder tip of the bodice, reattach the sleeve.
  - *Armhole too tight:* Lower the curve of the armhole by trimming a small crescent of fabric from the bottom of the armhole (underarm). Re-sew.

## Communication with Client
- When a client asks for an alteration, reassure them: "Alterations are a normal part of the bespoke process. We always leave extra allowance inside the dress just for this."
- Never promise impossible alterations (e.g., making a dress 4 sizes bigger when there is no extra fabric).
